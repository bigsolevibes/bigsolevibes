## Standing Orders

### The Shelf Gap — Top Priority

BSV is a head-to-toe brand. The shelf currently has zero dedicated foot care products. This is the single most important gap to close.

Every product-research run must hunt for foot care products first. Do not default to body care, face care, or grooming tools unless the foot care quota below is met.

What we need:
- Standard tier ($40-70): A professional-grade foot treatment. Fast-absorbing, active ingredients, men's or gender-neutral positioning, non-medicated. Think: what a serious podiatrist would put on the shelf next to a Merkur razor. Sources: specialist grooming retailers, European pharmacy lines, sports recovery brands. Go deep — if it shows up on the first page of Google it is not a discovery.
- Aspiration tier ($100-200): A foot care object with heritage. European craft provenance, the kind of thing that lives on a bathroom shelf and does not apologize for its price. German podiatry, French apothecary, Austrian alpine. It earns its place through story, not celebrity.

Both slots are open. Until both are filled with approved products scoring 70+, foot care is the first category briefed on every run.

### What Is Not on the Shelf

- Reverence de Bastien: HOLD. Do not research, evaluate, or score. Big D decision 2026-06-13.
- Camillen 60: REMOVED. Do not re-add.
- Any medicated, antifungal, or problem-positioned product: permanent exclusion.
- First-page Google results for "foot cream men": not a discovery. Go deeper.

### The Dual-Track Mandate

Track 1 (Premium transposition): Brands the BSV man has not been introduced to yet. European lines, professional-grade products sold through specialist channels.
Track 2 (Cultural crossover): Products already working in adjacent audiences (athletes, chefs, luxury spa) not yet claimed for the serious man's routine. A foot soak used by NBA training staff that nobody has marketed to men is Track 2.

Both tracks apply to foot care every week.

### Amazon First

All picks must have an Amazon ASIN and be available via tag bigsolevibes-20. No Amazon availability means hold until alternative affiliate channel is confirmed.

### The Voice Test

Before scoring any product: does this belong in a room with a Merkur razor and a Therabody WaveRoller? Yes = score it. No = move on.
