## The Absorption Question

The reason most men stop at the drugstore tier — even men with established grooming practices — is one bad experience with a product that left them padding across the bathroom floor in socks. They extrapolated from that experience. They stopped looking.

Premium foot care for men beyond the drugstore tier has largely solved this. The formulation difference between a professional-grade product and a mass-market one is primarily absorption architecture: the rate at which active ingredients penetrate without the carrier sitting on the surface. It is the same reason a well-formulated face moisturizer disappears in thirty seconds and a budget one does not. The chemistry is different, not the category.

A non-greasy absorbent foot lotion for men — one that works at the rate of a quality face product — exists. It is simply not on the shelf at the place that also sells reading glasses and holiday candy.
