## The Face

Cleanser first. Not bar soap — bar soap has a pH that strips the skin and leaves it working overtime to recover. A proper face wash takes thirty seconds and costs less than a round of drinks. It is the lowest-effort, highest-return change most men will ever make.

Moisturizer next, applied to slightly damp skin. Sixty seconds to absorb. No film, no residue. Just a face that looks like it belongs to a man who has his life in reasonable order.

---

## The Body

The shower gel is not decoration. What you wash with determines what your skin smells like before anything else goes on. If you're wearing a quality fragrance, you want the base layer to cooperate with it, not compete.

Two sprays of fragrance. Pulse points. Not clothes.
